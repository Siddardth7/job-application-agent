# Company Hiring Intel

Your own notes on how specific employers actually treat candidates like you — checked at
every shortlist. A company marked `SKIP` here is auto-dropped before scoring; `CAUTION`
adds a warning to its row.

This starts empty **on purpose**. It's worth more than any shipped list because it comes
from your own conversations, your own rejections, and job descriptions you've read
closely. Add a row whenever you learn something first-hand.

`/setup` copies this to `company_intel.md`, which is gitignored — these notes often come
from private conversations and should not be published.

### Actions

- **SKIP** — do not surface this employer again. Use for confirmed policies: no
  sponsorship, US-persons-only, a hiring freeze you've verified.
- **CAUTION** — surface it, but flag the row. Use when the policy varies by site,
  division, or posting, so you check the JD before applying.

### Writing a good row

Record the evidence, not the conclusion. "Recruiter said they don't sponsor" is weak in
six months; "Recruiter (name, date) said the Austin site has never sponsored for this job
family" is still usable. Where a JD is the source, quote the clause.
