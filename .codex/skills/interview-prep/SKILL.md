# Interview Prep

This is separate from `$apply-run`. Start from the exact submitted resume and JD. Read
`data/candidate_resume_database.json` and `profile.md`; use current verifiable company evidence when
research is requested. Build a role map, likely questions, grounded STAR bank, technical review,
questions for the interviewer, and a concise practice plan. Never invent experience, metrics,
interviewers, internal facts, or status. Do not send, schedule, or submit. If the submitted resume or
JD is missing, ask for that input.
